The Platform

Built for the arena.
Designed for everyone in it.

From bracket generation to live scoring to the parent on their phone in the stands — Gladio connects every part of the tournament in real time.

01Core

Real-Time Live Scoring

Every point, penalty, and decision pushed to all devices the instant it happens. No paper, no delay, no ambiguity.

02WKF

Brackets & Repechage

Full WKF Kumite bracket generation with automatic repechage logic. Single elimination, pools, bronze rounds — all handled.

03Fans

Follow Your Athlete

Follow competitors or your entire club. Push notifications for upcoming fights, live scores, and final results — from anywhere.

04Venue

Big-Screen Display Mode

Dedicated scoreboard view for venue screens. All tatamis simultaneously — brackets, live scores, upcoming matches.

05Admin

Federation-Grade Access

Role-based permissions for organizers, referees, coaches and spectators. Full audit trail. Built for national federations.

06Open

Open Source Core

Built openly. Any federation can audit, extend, or self-host. Community-driven development that evolves with the sport.

For Families & Fans

Your seat in the arena.
Wherever you are.

Follow your child, your teammate, your club. Get notified the moment their fight is called. Watch scores update live — from the other side of the hall or the other side of the country.

No app install required for spectators. Just a link, a phone, and the fight you don't want to miss.
